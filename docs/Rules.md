# Game Rules & Mechanics

## The Core Loop
1. **Name Your Area:** At the start of a new game, the player names their CBD area.
2. **Develop Land:** Start with an empty plot of land and accumulate enough funds to build the first building.
3. **Build & Expand:** Construct new buildings (Apartments, Food Courts, Parking, Office Towers, Malls).
4. **Floor Management:** Within each building, construct and configure specific floors (underground and above ground) and fill them with appropriate facilities or tenants.
5. **Earn & Reinvest:** Collect rent from tenants and services, then reinvest into more expensive buildings like Malls and Office Towers.

## Economy & Currency
- **Soft Currency (Coins):** Earned through rent. Used for construction, expanding floors, and basic upgrades.
- **Hard Currency (Gems):** Earned via achievements (no online monetization dependencies). Used to speed up wait times or buy premium decorations.

## Building & Floor Mechanics
- **Building Types:**
  - *Apartments:* Steady residential income, medium cost.
  - *Food Courts:* High traffic, good early-to-mid game income.
  - *Parking Areas:* Low income but provides necessary capacity/support for other buildings.
  - *Office Towers:* Very expensive, massive late-game income.
  - *Malls:* Very expensive, high retail revenue.
- **Floor System:**
  - Buildings can be expanded vertically.
  - **Underground Floors:** Primarily for parking, utilities, or basement storage.
  - **Above Ground Floors:** For residential, commercial, or office spaces.

## Tenant Management
- Players must actively manage tenants on each floor. 
- Different tenants have different needs (e.g., Office tenants need parking; Residential tenants need quiet).
- If tenant needs are not met, happiness and rent decrease, and they may leave.

## Offline Progression
- The game fundamentally relies on offline idle mechanics. Resource generation continues seamlessly while the app is closed, awarding players retroactively when they launch the game based entirely on local progression.
