# Game Rules & Mechanics

## The Core Loop
1. **Earn Money:** Collect rent from existing tenants and services over time.
2. **Invest:** Spend money to build new rooms, upgrade existing ones, or hire staff.
3. **Attract:** Better rooms and services attract more paying tenants.
4. **Repeat:** Higher income leads to further expansion.

## Economy & Currency
- **Soft Currency (Coins):** Earned through rent. Used for basic construction and upgrades.
- **Hard Currency (Gems):** Earned via achievements or IAP. Used to speed up wait times or buy premium decorations.

## Building Mechanics
- **Room Types:**
  - Residential (Apartments, Penthouses)
  - Commercial (Cafes, Gyms, Laundromats)
  - Utility (Power generators, Water pumps)
- **Adjacency Bonuses:** Placing certain rooms next to each other (e.g., a Gym next to a Smoothie Bar) can yield a revenue bonus.

## Tenant Needs
- Tenants require power and water. If utility capacity is exceeded, some rooms will shut down.
- Noise pollution: Placing loud commercial rooms next to residential rooms may decrease tenant happiness and rent.
