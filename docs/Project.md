# Project Overview

## Title
Building Management Tycoon Game

## Concept
A 2D offline simulation and management tycoon game where players build, upgrade, and manage various properties to generate revenue, keep tenants happy, and expand their real estate empire.

## Core Objectives
- **Build & Expand:** Construct new rooms, facilities, and amenities in a 2D space.
- **Manage Resources:** Balance income (rent, services) and expenses (maintenance, staff, utilities).
- **Tenant Happiness:** Ensure tenants have their needs met to maximize rent and prevent them from leaving.
- **Progression:** Unlock new building types, advanced facilities, and city areas as the player levels up.

## Target Audience
Casual to mid-core players who enjoy simulation, strategy, and purely offline idle-tycoon mechanics.

## Scope
- Minimum Viable Product (MVP): A single building with basic rooms, a few tenant types, and core offline economy loop, playable on Web and Android.
- Future Updates: Multiple buildings, VIP tenants, events, and deeper customization.
