# Volumetric city figures and completed-storey exteriors

User request, 2026-10-17: characters look flat; make them 3D, and make upward building upgrades increase exterior height.

## Decisions

The old map used side-facing raster actor sheets with horizontal flips and an affine-sheared car. Near a vertical screen heading, the car wheelbase could collapse. Putting every building above every actor also clipped people walking in front of a facade. Replace outdoor billboards with actual XYZ meshes: articulated trouser/arm segments, torso, faceted round head/hair, facial features and shoes; cars have body/cabin, glazing, wheels and lights. Each role keeps its city palette. World-heading rotation, face normals, lighting, backface culling and face-depth sorting retain volume in every direction. These are lightweight procedural models rendered in the fixed isometric camera, not imported GLB/rigged assets or an orbiting 3D engine. Indoor approved sprites remain intact.

The independent 30 Hz presentation clock does not alter simulation time, income or saved data. Freeze under reduced motion; suspend hidden maps. Up to 12 visible businesses show two pedestrians and one car each. Routes and floor-aware building silhouettes are cached and invalidated when scene geometry changes. Each small actor layer erases pixels behind nearer building silhouettes with dstOut, avoiding compound inverse-clip differences across rendering backends. Traffic is drawn after structures, clipping only buildings between its world position and the camera; native building labels remain above traffic. A front-side pedestrian retains its head/body instead of being cut off by its own building. This remains cosmetic traffic without collision AI or destination simulation.

Each approved exterior PNG supplies a ground-floor strip and repeatable upper-storey bays. Apartment/hotel source faces contain four illustrated storeys, office five, food court one. Upper floors repeat one suitable source strip rather than stretching the whole picture. Every plot, including square plots, now has actual footprint-shaped walls and one roof at completedFloors × storey height. A storey has a consistent world height on every plot: 0.34 projected tile (0.46 for food courts). Outdoor people render at 0.72 model scale so an adult fits within one storey. These are visual dimensions, not economic values. Preserve roof/cream trim and service-unit proportions. Bounds, silhouette picking, activity culling and occlusion share the floor-aware projection.

The saved completed count is authoritative: an unfinished/canceled floor order does not raise the building; domain completion adds one visible storey. Basement/lift/luxury upgrades do not increase height. The ground anchor, width, immutable land and road network remain fixed. Existing 1–10 floor saves update their presentation on load without a migration or charge. Schema 8/catalog 2, five slots, 24-second days, approved values and game limits remain unchanged. No new runtime binaries, packages or large art sources are added.
