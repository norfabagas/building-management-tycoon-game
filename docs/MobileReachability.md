# Small-device reachability

Implemented 2026-10-17 in response to inaccessible popups/buttons, especially building review.

## Layout decisions

The 2026-10-26 activities update places a labelled trophy Activities menu at the fixed left edge of the district toolbar, before the independently scrolling actions. Both its trigger and menu rows retain 48px targets. See [ActivitiesNavigation.md](ActivitiesNavigation.md); this supersedes the former far-right icon placement.

- Final construction review takes the content area instead of competing with the map. Hide the redundant district HUD/navigation during this step. Scroll the rules, dimensions, floor count, B1 capacity, cost breakdown, revenue and upkeep independently; pin the current total, balance after purchase, confirm, cancel and redraw actions. Short landscape puts these three actions in one row to recover reading space. The domain still validates at commit. Cancel/redraw never charge the player.
- During land drawing, keep Review cost pinned below scrollable planning details. Narrow portrait allocates 32% of the body to its detail panel. Short landscape at widths from 500 uses a side panel. Hide unrelated district navigation during construction/decorating. The camera and preview remain mounted for redraw/resize.
- On narrow portrait phones use a two-row HUD with district/weekday and balance/day-clock/gems, allocating more width to large money values. Short landscape uses a horizontally scrollable compact HUD. The district toolbar is one horizontally scrollable row with a visible scrollbar; District activities is fixed. On short narrow landscape the welcome sidebar collapses, while explicit directories/planning remain available. Existing wide-screen welcome lists remain present.
- Keep Exit building, Go up and Go down together with 48px minimum touch targets and smaller 14px labels/padding. Do not shrink touch targets to squeeze text. Above selected-floor navigation, content still scrolls independently. In short landscape, replace the separate HUD with the current floor and live balance beside the same navigation buttons; this recovers content height without hiding the post-purchase balance.
- On maps narrower than 400px and shorter than 260px, arrange the same zoom controls vertically along the right edge. This prevents the previous horizontal zoom bar intercepting the central starting plot. Projection, touch picking and camera rules remain unchanged.
- Shared GameReviewDialog replaces upgrade, acceleration, profile creation/deletion AlertDialogs. The cream outlined game shell has a maximum width of 560px, 12px margins, keyboard-aware Dialog layout and existing Web footer clearance. Title/details/form share one bounded scroll view; actions remain outside it, use at least 48px height and stack on narrow portrait screens. Short landscape keeps actions side by side. Existing confirmation labels, retry behavior, asynchronous commit guards and deletion warnings remain.
- Tight profile cards stack their text and actions below 280px of card space. The custom full-page tenant management review remains in place; its short-landscape action pair uses one row, with descriptive context in the scrollable body.

## Scope and limitations

No asset references/bytes, BM master, economy values, road connectivity rules, save schema, slot count or phase timing changes. All data remains local; no network dependency or account is added. Browser viewport QA is not a physical phone/Android or soft-keyboard certification. Close/reopen old game tabs to activate the offline pack without clearing site data.
